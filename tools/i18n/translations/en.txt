tip_1_title = Space it out
tip_1_body = Several short sessions across the week beat one long cram. Spacing is one of the most replicated findings in learning science.
tip_2_title = Test yourself
tip_2_body = After reading, close the book and write down what you remember. Retrieval strengthens memory far more than re-reading.
tip_3_title = Mix it up
tip_3_body = Interleave topics or problem types instead of drilling one at a time. It feels harder, and that's why it works.
tip_4_title = Teach it back
tip_4_body = Explain the idea in plain words as if to a curious 12-year-old. Gaps in your explanation are gaps in your understanding.
tip_5_title = Decide when and where
tip_5_body = "I'll learn at 7 pm at the kitchen table" makes you far more likely to follow through than "I'll get to it tonight."
tip_6_title = Sleep on it
tip_6_body = Sleep consolidates what you learned during the day. A late-night cram often costs more than it gains.
tip_7_title = Aim just past comfortable
tip_7_body = Pick practice that's slightly too hard. If it feels easy, you're rehearsing, not learning.
tip_8_title = One-line reflection
tip_8_body = Ending a session with "one thing I learned" is a tiny retrieval exercise that also shows you your progress.
tip_9_title = Guard your focus
tip_9_body = Put your phone in another room for a focus block. Even a phone face-down on the desk can drain attention.
tip_10_title = Connect it
tip_10_body = Ask "how does this relate to something I already know?" Linked ideas are easier to recall later.
tip_11_title = Go easy on misses
tip_11_body = Missing a day doesn't break a habit. What matters is getting back on track the next day.
tip_12_title = Words + pictures
tip_12_body = Sketch a quick diagram of what you're learning. Combining visuals with words gives memory two paths in.
tip_13_title = Ask why
tip_13_body = Keep asking "why is this true?" as you read. Self-explanation turns facts into understanding.
tip_14_title = Finish with a plan
tip_14_body = End each session by noting where to start next time. Starting is the hardest part — make it easy for future you.
